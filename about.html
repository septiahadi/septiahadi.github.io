About

Hi!, I’m a Geological Engineering graduate passionate about geoscience, data analytics, visualization, and AI. My journey started in mining exploration, GIS, and geological analysis, where I also gained experience in startup building through national programs.

Discovering AI became a turning point for me—it changed how I learn, structure my growth, and work on projects. From building my website to managing workflows, AI has helped me move faster and think more systematically.

Beyond the technical side, I’ve always had a strong sense of design. That’s why I enjoy transforming data into visuals and creative outputs, blending analysis with storytelling. For me, visualization and creativity are just as important as accuracy, because they make science easier to understand and share.
