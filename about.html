Septia Hadi Wardana

I am a professional with a background in Geological Engineering, deeply passionate about geospatial data analysis, data visualization, creative design, and artificial intelligence.

My career journey began as an engineer in mining exploration, GIS, and geological analysis. Over time, I shifted my focus toward data analytics, visualization, and AI, fields that I now pursue intensively. Along the way, I also co-founded a startup and served as a hustler, where our team successfully reached the Top 5 at the 1001Startup Regional Kalimantan program in Balikpapan.

I am driven by a passion for building impactful things. The rise of AI has transformed how I learn, work, and create—it allows me to study faster, structure my growth, build my website, and even run my current business, YADA Certify, more effectively.

Beyond tech, I’ve always had a strong sense of art and design. This is why I dedicate time to exploring the intersection of data, visualization, and creativity. To manage the variety of projects and research I am undertaking, I founded Synthetic—a virtual office and research lab where I document my projects and share my learnings. Through Synthetic, I aspire not only to grow myself but also to mentor and inspire others.
